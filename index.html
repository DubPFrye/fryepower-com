About FryePower

FryePower is a hobby, not a shop. I build and fix my own e-bikes, and when I make something useful along the way (a tool, a part, a set of settings) I put it here so other builders don't have to start from scratch.

Everything is free to use, at your own risk. These are works in progress from a home workshop, not certified products, and lithium-ion batteries deserve real care.
